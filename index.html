Privacy Policy

Expense Tracker · Last updated 5 October 2026

Expense Tracker is a personal budgeting app. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not collect.

Your financial data stays on your phone

Every transaction, category and budget you enter is stored in a single file in the app's private storage on your device. It is never transmitted to us, never uploaded to a server, and never shared with any third party. We operate no servers and hold no copy of it.

There is no account and no sign-in. We cannot see your data, and neither can anyone else with the app.

What advertising collects

The app displays banner ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve those ads, Google's SDK may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • approximate location, derived from your IP address (not GPS)
  • basic device and app information, such as device model and OS version

This is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. It is the only information that leaves your device, and it never includes your transactions, amounts, categories or notes.

Consent

Where legally required — the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland — the app shows a consent form before requesting personalised ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change that choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy options.

Anywhere else, no consent form is shown because none is required.

Your control

  • Export — Settings offers a full JSON backup and a CSV export.
  • Delete — Settings can erase all transactions. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes everything, since the only copy is on your phone.
  • Opt out of personalised ads — through your Android device settings, under Privacy → Ads.
Because your data exists only on your device, we cannot restore it for you. Export a backup before changing phones or reinstalling.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
